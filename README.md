# Construction HR Analytics Dashboard | Microsoft Excel

## Project Overview

The Construction HR Analytics project uses Microsoft Excel to analyse human resources data and generate insights into workforce management, payroll costs, attrition risk, attendance, training, recruitment, and workplace safety.

The dataset covers **1,000 employees across 17 departments and 11 countries**.

The main objective is to transform raw HR data into meaningful insights that support better decision-making, improve workforce planning, and identify areas requiring management attention.

## Objectives

- Analyse workforce distribution and employee costs.
- Identify departments and employee groups with higher attrition risk.
- Examine attendance patterns, working hours, and overtime.
- Evaluate training participation, training expenditure, and employee performance.
- Analyse recruitment sources, application stages, and salary offers.
- Investigate workplace incidents, root causes, lost working days, and incident costs.

## Tools and Techniques

- **Microsoft Excel**
- Data cleaning and standardization
- Missing-value and duplicate checks
- Date-format correction
- Calculated columns and data validation
- Pivot tables and pivot charts
- KPI cards and interactive dashboards
- HR metrics and business analysis

## Dashboards

### 1. Workforce & Payroll Overview
Analyses employee headcount, salary costs, payroll trends, overtime payments, employment types, and visa status.

### 2. Attrition Analysis
Examines high- and critical-risk employee groups across departments, countries, employment types, tenure groups, and performance bands.

### 3. Attendance & Workforce Productivity
Evaluates attendance status, working hours, shifts, work locations, and departmental overtime.

### 4. Training & Performance Analysis
Explores training participation, training costs, pass rates, performance ratings, bonuses, and salary increments.

### 5. Recruitment Analysis
Examines applications by department, recruitment sources, recruitment stages, monthly trends, time to hire, and offered salaries.

### 6. Safety Analysis
Analyses incident severity, monthly incident trends, incident locations, lost working days, reported root causes, and incident costs.

## Key Insights

- Identified departments with comparatively higher reported attrition risk.
- Observed variations in payroll costs and overtime across departments and months.
- Identified attendance patterns and departments with higher recorded leave.
- Compared training expenditure and participation across training programmes.
- Identified LinkedIn and employee referrals as important recruitment sources.
- Highlighted frequently reported workplace incident causes and departments requiring further investigation.

These findings indicate areas for further investigation and potential improvements in HR planning, employee retention, operational efficiency, and workplace safety.
